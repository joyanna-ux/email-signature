Installing it

Classic Outlook (Windows) — most reliable: click Download .htm, then put the file in %APPDATA%\Microsoft\Signatures\. Restart Outlook and pick it under File → Options → Mail → Signatures. Alternative: Copy for Outlook, open that same Signatures dialog, click into the editor, and paste.

New Outlook, Outlook for Mac, Outlook on the web — Copy for Outlook, then Settings → Accounts → Signatures, click into the editor, paste.

Why it breaks when edited: Outlook re-saves the signature through Word’s HTML engine, which rewrites spacing and drops styles. Any change — even fixing a digit — should be made here and re-copied.
